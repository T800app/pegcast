Privacy Policy

Your data under UK law — explained simply

1. Who we are & contact

PegCast ("we", "us") is a free UK-based laundry drying tool. We handle personal data in compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Data controller contact: whichischeaperapp@gmail.com. The UK supervisory authority is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

2. What data we collect

  • No account required. You can use the site without sharing any personal data.
  • City / location query: the city or postcode you type, or approximate coordinates if you tap "My location", is used only to fetch weather data and is not stored on our servers. Saved locations are stored only in your browser (localStorage).

3. Legal basis (Art. 6 UK GDPR)

The site uses no advertising or analytics cookies and no consent-based tracking. Your saved locations and preferences (such as dark mode) are stored only in your browser (localStorage) to provide the service; this data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or to any third party. Weather results are fetched on demand only to display the forecast you requested.

5. International transfers

GitHub Pages (static hosting), Cloudflare (weather proxy) and WeatherAPI.com may process data outside the UK while serving this site. Transfers are covered by the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) and Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) where applicable. This site creates no advertising profiles or tracking identifiers.

6. Data retention

  • Saved locations and settings: stored only in your browser (localStorage); deleted when you clear site data or tap Reset in Settings.
  • Weather queries: not stored on our servers; forecasts are cached briefly in your browser only to reduce repeat requests.

7. Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights email whichischeaperapp@gmail.com. If you are unhappy with our response, you may complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).

8. Children, security & changes

Our service is not directed at children under 13. We use HTTPS and secure hosting. We may update this policy; material changes will be posted here. Last updated November 2026.
