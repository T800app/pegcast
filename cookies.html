1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences. We also use browser local storage (saved only on your device) to remember your saved locations, dark mode preference and cookie consent choice. In the UK, the use of non-essential cookies is governed by the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR), which requires your consent.

2. How we use consent (Consent Mode v2)

We use Google Consent Mode v2. Before you accept, all advertising and analytics cookies are blocked by default (four signals: ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization, analytics_storage = denied). Clicking Accept sets them to granted and allows Google Analytics and Google AdSense cookies. Clicking Decline keeps them denied. Your choice is saved for 180 days in your browser only. To change it later, clear your browser cookies for this site.

4. How to manage or delete cookies

  • Clear site data in your browser: Chrome / Firefox / Safari / Edge → Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies / Site data → remove pegcast.co.uk.
  • Opt out of personalised advertising: Your Online Choices · Google Ads Settings.
  • For advice on your rights, contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
